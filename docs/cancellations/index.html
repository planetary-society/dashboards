About This Dashboard

This dashboard tracks NASA contracts and grants terminated since January 20, 2025. It is maintained by Casey Dreier at The Planetary Society to provide transparency into changes affecting NASA's programs and the organizations that support them.

The dashboard presents two independent datasets, selected at the top of the Dashboard tab. They answer different questions and may overlap — many awards claimed by DOGE also appear among the confirmed terminations — so their counts must never be added together.

Confirmed Cancellations

NASA awards whose federal record shows a termination action. Each award was identified from USAspending.gov award data in one of two ways: a formal FPDS termination action code (98 of the 177 listed awards carry one), or explicit termination language in the text of the award's transaction descriptions (79 awards rest on description text alone). Both are records the government itself filed; the difference is whether the termination was coded or only described.

The headline count excludes five awards where the federal record shows a partial action rather than a full termination: three descoped awards (part of the work was cut while the award continues) and two closed-out awards. They appear in the table with their own labels, and their dollars are excluded from the panel's totals.

DOGE Claims

Every NASA award cancellation publicly claimed by the Department of Government Efficiency. DOGE is no longer active; this dataset is preserved as a historical record of its claims and their outcomes. Each claim was checked against the award's federal transaction history, and the outcome shown follows the federal record, not DOGE's label: some awards DOGE listed as “expired” show explicit termination actions, and some claims could not be matched to any federal award at all.

Claimed savings figures are DOGE's own numbers, reproduced as claimed. Most claims list no savings figure, and some claimed savings are attached to awards that remain active — the dashboard states both alongside any total.

What's an Award?

In federal spending, an award is money the government commits to a recipient. There are two main types:

  • Contracts — Agreements to purchase specific goods or services. NASA uses contracts to buy spacecraft, fund research and development, and hire technical support.
  • Grants — Financial assistance to support a public purpose. NASA awards grants to universities, nonprofits, and researchers for scientific studies, education programs, and technology development.

The dollar amounts shown represent obligations—money the government committed to spend—which may differ from amounts actually paid out. Counts on this dashboard are counts of awards, not organizations: a single university or company can hold several awards.

Why This Matters

NASA awards billions of dollars annually to universities, research institutions, and private companies across the United States. When contracts or grants are terminated, it can affect scientific research, student training, small businesses, and local economies. This dashboard helps citizens, journalists, and policymakers track these changes.

About the Fiscal-Year Chart

The fiscal-year chart on the Confirmed Cancellations panel counts FPDS “termination for convenience” contract actions across all of NASA. It is drawn from a different, and narrower, records universe than the panel's award list: only contracts receive FPDS action codes, so grants and description-detected terminations do not appear in it. Federal fiscal years run October through September, so the FY2025 bar includes actions from October 2024 through January 19, 2025 (roughly 9 of its 74 actions) that predate the current administration. The full FY2010–2026 series is included in the downloadable file.

Data Sources & Downloads

  • USAspending.gov — The authoritative federal spending database. All award details (recipient names, funding amounts, dates, locations, congressional districts) come from this source.
  • DOGE's published list — The savings claims posted by the Department of Government Efficiency for NASA contracts and grants, archived before DOGE's dissolution.

Download the data behind each panel:

  • terminations.csv — Confirmed Cancellations (one row per award termination action)
  • doge_claims.csv — DOGE Claims (one row per claim, with verification columns)
  • cancellations_for_convenience_actions_by_fiscal_year.csv — FPDS convenience-termination actions by fiscal year, FY2010–2026

Important Notes

  • Updated daily — The source data is refreshed from federal records each day; the date of the last update is shown at the top of the Dashboard tab.
  • Not exhaustive — This list captures awards meeting specific detection criteria. Cancellations that leave no trace in these sources will not appear.
  • Counts are not comparable to earlier versions of this page — The dashboard moved to a rebuilt data pipeline in August 2026 with different detection criteria than earlier versions.
  • Further verification recommended — Inclusion reflects evidence in federal data. Before publishing, confirm details with the recipient or with NASA; corrections are welcome.
