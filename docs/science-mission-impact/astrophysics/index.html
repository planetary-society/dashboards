Astrophysics

NASA's Astrophysics Division is responsible for the study of the universe, primarily using space telescopes.

Scope Active Mission WindowActivePapers published from the first full month after science operations begin through two years after the mission ends. Extended missions are not included. MethodsTracked publications: peer-reviewed papers we found that name the mission or its instruments in their titles, abstracts, or keywords. Papers that used the data without naming it are missed. MethodsCitations: how often other papers cited these publications. Each paper’s citations count over the year it appeared and the three years after, so older missions get no head start.
