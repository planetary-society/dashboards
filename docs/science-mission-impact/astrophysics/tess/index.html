How we found these papers

We searched NASA’s Astrophysics Data System (ADS) for peer-reviewed articles naming TESS in the title, abstract or keywords; standard filters drop articles that are not peer-reviewed and magazine pieces. SciX is ADS’s current interface.

((=abs:"Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite" OR (abs:TESS AND abs:(mission OR satellite OR spacecraft OR catalog OR exoplanet OR planet OR star OR stellar OR photometry OR photometric OR asteroseismology OR transit OR pulsation OR binary OR supernova OR galaxy OR asteroid OR comet OR Jupiter)) OR facility:TESS))
