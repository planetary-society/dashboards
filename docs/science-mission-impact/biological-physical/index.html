Biological & Physical Sciences

Biological and Physical Sciences studies how living things and physical processes behave in space. Most of its research runs aboard the International Space Station, outside this study of free-flying missions. The missions here are CubeSats, each testing how bacteria, yeast, plant cells, or organic molecules respond to weightlessness or space radiation.

5 missions in this study, with 15 tracked publications and 237 citations to date.
