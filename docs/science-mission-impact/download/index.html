Data dictionary

CSV files use UTF-8, a header row, and comma-separated fields with standard CSV quoting. Row counts exclude the header.

The all-publications file is a ZIP holding one CSV. Its manifest row count describes that CSV; its size and checksum describe the ZIP.

Mission summaries have one row per mission. Publication exports have one row per mission and paper: a paper associated with two missions appears twice. Within a mission, bibcode identifies a publication.

CLPS exports have one row per lander and paper, or one row with blank paper fields for a lander with no papers. The lander’s lifetime_papers repeats on each of its rows, so do not sum it; zero is measured no output and blank is unavailable.

An empty CSV field or JSON null is missing, unavailable, outside the applicable window, or not measured; it is not zero. A numeric zero is recorded output or an explicitly assumed failure zero, as explained on the mission page.

Compare citation ranks within a science division. Publication cultures differ; these measures do not rank the overall scientific value of different fields.

Publication-level ranking weights precede mission sharing and can be fractional. Mission summary high-impact credit already includes that sharing; do not divide it again.

Active-window sharing counts are not exported. Dividing top10_weight_full or top1_weight_full by shared_by does not reproduce active-window mission credits; use the authoritative active_top10 and active_top1 summary fields.

Files at these URLs are replaced by later snapshots. The manifest version identifies the current export contents; these URLs are not a historical archive.

Measurement scopes

Active Mission Window. Mission-specific publication windows, from the month after science starts through the month 2 years after the mission ends (never shorter than 2 years), cut at the latest date that is mature on November 6, 2026. Exact exported boundaries are shown on each mission page. Each paper’s citations cover its publication year and the following 3 calendar years. Internal JSON uses “full” for this scope.

Lifetime. Every tracked publication and every recorded citation through the 2026-11-06 snapshot. The lifetime scope reports papers and citations only, with no top-10% or top-1% measure: lifetime counts favor older papers, so high-impact comparisons use the Active Mission Window.

Fields

CSV columns, and the columns of each mission’s publication JSON, which names them the same way.

division
NASA science division slug. Use with mission to identify a mission–paper association.
mission
Upstream mission short title, used as the join key across CSV files. The manifest also supplies the site’s mission ID and page URLs.
name
Mission display name.
launch_year
Catalog launch year; blank when unknown.
type
Catalog mission type.
lcc
Reported prime-mission life-cycle cost, in millions of US dollars before inflation adjustment. Includes documented partner contributions; excludes extended-mission spending.
adjusted_lcc
Prime-mission life-cycle cost in millions of 2025 US dollars.
status
Catalog mission status, including full or partial failures where recorded.
selection
Catalog selection strategy as exported.
formulation_start
Catalog project formulation start date, YYYY-MM-DD when known.
launch_date
Catalog launch date, YYYY-MM-DD when known.
science_start
Catalog start of science operations, YYYY-MM-DD when known.
active_window_start
Exported start boundary of the mission-specific Active Mission Window, YYYY-MM-DD.
active_window_end
Exported end boundary of the mission-specific Active Mission Window, YYYY-MM-DD.
active_papers
Tracked publications in the Active Mission Window.
active_citations
Citations to active-window publications over each paper’s publication year and the following 3 calendar years.
active_top10
Active-window top-10% publication credit within the division. Fractional credit accounts for ties and papers shared by missions.
active_top1
Active-window top-1% publication credit within the division. Fractional credit accounts for ties and papers shared by missions.
lifetime_papers
Every tracked publication associated with the mission as of the snapshot.
lifetime_citations
All recorded citations to the mission’s tracked publications as of the snapshot.
h_index
Lifetime h-index: the largest h for which h tracked papers each have at least h citations.
g_index
Lifetime g-index: the largest g for which the g most cited tracked papers have at least g² citations in total.
url
Canonical active-window mission page URL.
bibcode
SciX/ADS publication identifier. Deduplicate by bibcode when counting distinct publications across missions.
year
Publication year from SciX/ADS; may differ from the year encoded in the bibcode.
pubdate
SciX/ADS publication date as a string. Zero month or day means unknown precision (for example 2003-01-00); do not parse as an ordinary ISO calendar date.
title
Publication title with source markup removed.
first_author
First author supplied by SciX/ADS, with source markup removed.
citations
All recorded citations to this publication as of the snapshot.
full_window_citations
Citations to this publication in its Active Mission Window citation horizon (4 calendar years including publication year). Blank means outside the mission’s window or unavailable.
top10_weight_full
Per-publication active-window top-10% tie weight in the division, before dividing credit among missions sharing the paper. Blank means outside the window or unavailable.
top1_weight_full
Per-publication active-window top-1% tie weight in the division, before dividing credit among missions sharing the paper. Blank means outside the window or unavailable.
shared_by
Number of missions in the division associated with this publication in the lifetime cohort. This can differ from active-window sharing; it is not the denominator for reconstructing active-window high-impact credit.
scix_url
Direct link to the publication record in NASA Science Explorer.
curated
Publication JSON only: 1 for a paper added by human-reviewed curation rather than returned by the query; 0 for one the query returned.
