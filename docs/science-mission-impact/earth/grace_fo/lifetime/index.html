Gravity Recovery and Climate Experiment-Follow On (GRACE-FO)

Orbiter · Directed · Agency-led · Launched on May 22, 2018 · Extended Mission · LCC: $521M/$694M (2025 $)Life-cycle cost: NASA’s reported cost to build, launch, and operate a mission through its prime mission, plus documented partner contributions, in 2025 dollars. Extended missions are not included. Methods · Wikipedia

This mission is not yet included in the analysis because its publication and citation windows have not matured for comparison. Expected inclusion date: Oct. 1, 2028. Only lifetime data is shown. Maturity rules.

Tracked publicationsTracked publications: peer-reviewed papers we found that name the mission or its instruments in their titles, abstracts, or keywords. Papers that used the data without naming it are missed. Methods980
CitationsCitations: every citation these publications have drawn to date. Older missions have had longer to collect them. Methods13,467
Citation indices
h-indexh-index: the largest h such that h papers have at least h citations each. It only grows with time, so older missions score higher. Methods48
g-indexg-index: the largest g for which the g most-cited papers together hold at least g² citations. Like the h-index, but it lets the most-cited papers count for more. Methods86
m-indexm-index: a mission’s h-index divided by the years since its first peer-reviewed paper. It falls every Jan. 1, even when nothing else changes; the figure shown is as of Nov. 18, 2026. It also discounts the long operating life that larger missions paid for. Methods5.3
toritori (total research impact, from SciX): for every paper citing one of the mission’s papers, 1 divided by the citing paper’s reference count times the cited paper’s author count, summed, with self-citations removed. It favors citations from papers with short reference lists and from outside the mission’s own authors. Computed over the tracked citation graph, which can be slightly incomplete. Always over every tracked publication to date, whichever scope is chosen. Methods65
riqriq (research impact quotient): 1,000 times the square root of tori, divided by the years since the mission’s first paper. A rate, not a total, so it does not keep growing with age the way the h-index and tori do. Like tori, always over every tracked publication to date. Methods806
