Earth Science

Earth Science watches the planet’s air, oceans, land, and ice from orbit, often for decades, with carefully calibrated instruments. NASA directs most of its larger missions, such as Terra, Aqua, and the Landsat series, to keep long records unbroken. A few mid-size missions, such as CloudSat and CYGNSS, were competed. Most of its small missions are CubeSats that test new instrument technology.

41 missions in this study, with 46,444 tracked publications and 1,524,194 citations to date.

Small and larger missions

Small missions, those that cost $150 million or less, make up 32% of Earth Science’s missions in this study and 0.43% of its spending. They produced 0.05% of its top-10% papers, the ones other scientists cited most. Larger missions also drew more citations per dollar.
