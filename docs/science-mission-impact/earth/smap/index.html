How we found these papers

We searched NASA’s Astrophysics Data System (ADS) for peer-reviewed articles naming SMAP in the title, abstract or keywords; standard filters drop articles that are not peer-reviewed and magazine pieces. SciX is ADS’s current interface.

((=abs:"Soil Moisture Active Passive" OR =abs:"SMAP Reflectometry" OR (abs:SMAP AND (abs:(satellite OR soil OR radiometer OR microwave OR ocean OR freeze OR thaw OR vegetation OR drought OR SAR) OR =abs:salinity OR =abs:"remote sensing" OR =abs:"land surface" OR =abs:"gap filling" OR abs:"L-band"))) AND NOT =abs:"GIC-SMAP")
