How we found these papers

We searched NASA’s Astrophysics Data System (ADS) for peer-reviewed articles naming SORCE in the title, abstract or keywords; standard filters drop articles that are not peer-reviewed and magazine pieces. SciX is ADS’s current interface.

((=abs:"Solar Radiation and Climate Experiment" OR (abs:"SORCE" AND abs:(solar OR irradiance))))
