How we found these papers

We searched NASA’s Astrophysics Data System (ADS) for peer-reviewed articles naming TOMS-EP in the title, abstract or keywords; standard filters drop articles that are not peer-reviewed and magazine pieces. SciX is ADS’s current interface.

(((=abs:"Earth Probe" AND (abs:TOMS OR =abs:"Total Ozone Mapping Spectrometer")) OR ((abs:"TOMS-EP" OR abs:"EP-TOMS" OR abs:EPTOMS) AND abs:(ozone OR aerosol OR ultraviolet OR UV OR SO2 OR volcanic OR satellite))))
