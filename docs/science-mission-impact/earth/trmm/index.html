TRMM

3,030 tracked publications and 127,638 citations from 1998–2026. Tropical Rainfall Measuring Mission. Launched 1997. Life-cycle cost: $2.4B in 2025 dollars. Active Mission Window January 1, 1998 to May 1, 2017: 1,634 publications, 159 top-10% credit.

h-indexh-index: the largest h such that h papers have at least h citations each. It only grows with time, so older missions score higher. Methods144144
g-indexg-index: the largest g for which the g most-cited papers together hold at least g² citations. Like the h-index, but it lets the most-cited papers count for more. Methods247247
m-index, as of October 4, 2026m-index: a mission’s h-index divided by the years since its first peer-reviewed paper. It falls every 1 January even when nothing else changes, so it belongs to the date shown; it also discounts the long operating life that larger missions paid for. Methods5.05.0
toritori (total research impact, from ADS): for every paper citing one of the mission’s papers, 1 divided by the citing paper’s reference count times the cited paper’s author count, summed, with self-citations removed. It favors citations from papers with short reference lists and from outside the mission’s own authors. Computed over the tracked citation graph, which can be slightly incomplete. Methods815815
riqriq (research impact quotient): 1,000 times the square root of tori, divided by the years since the mission’s first paper. A rate, not a total, so it does not keep growing with age the way the h-index and tori do. Methods984984

How we found these papers

We searched NASA’s Astrophysics Data System (ADS) for peer-reviewed articles naming TRMM in the title, abstract or keywords; standard filters drop articles that are not peer-reviewed and magazine pieces. SciX is ADS’s current interface.
