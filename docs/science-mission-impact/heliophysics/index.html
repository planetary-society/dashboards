Heliophysics

Heliophysics studies the Sun and the space it fills, from the solar wind to the magnetic field and upper atmosphere around Earth. Some missions image the Sun or Earth’s surroundings from a distance; others measure the particles and fields they fly through, sometimes with several spacecraft at once. Most are Explorers or CubeSats led by a principal investigator.

33 missions in this study, with 18,310 tracked publications and 592,858 citations to date.

Small and larger missions

Small missions, those that cost $150 million or less, make up 48% of Heliophysics’ missions in this study and 5.0% of its spending. They produced 8.3% of its top-10% papers, the ones other scientists cited most. TRACE alone produced 69% of that share. Per dollar, small missions drew more citations, but only because of TRACE; without it, larger missions lead.
