How we found these papers

We searched NASA’s Astrophysics Data System (ADS) for peer-reviewed articles naming MMS in the title, abstract or keywords; standard filters drop articles that are not peer-reviewed and magazine pieces. SciX is ADS’s current interface.

((=abs:"Magnetospheric Multiscale" OR =abs:"Magnetospheric Multi-scale" OR (abs:MMS AND abs:(magnetos* OR magnetopause OR magnetotail OR "magnetic reconnection" OR "plasma sheet" OR "current sheet" OR "bow shock" OR "solar wind" OR whistler OR chorus))))

Standard filters
