How we found these papers

We searched NASA’s Astrophysics Data System (ADS) for peer-reviewed articles naming SNOE in the title, abstract or keywords; standard filters drop articles that are not peer-reviewed and magazine pieces. SciX is ADS’s current interface.

((=abs:"Student Nitric Oxide Explorer" OR ((abs:SNOE) AND abs:("nitric oxide" OR thermosphere OR mesosphere OR "soft X-ray" OR ionosphere OR auroral))))
