Planetary Science

Planetary Science sends spacecraft to explore destinations in our solar system, from the Moon to beyond Pluto.

Scope Papers published from the first full month after science operations begin through two years after the mission ends. Citations are counted through the third calendar year after each paper appears. Methods/ Every tracked publication to date, with every citation to date. Methods

The running average combines mission rates across a broad range of nearby costs, giving closer missions more weight. Missions with no citations remain included and sit on the bottom row. Shading shows a 95% interval for the average at each cost. Methods

ImageLife-cycle cost: NASA’s reported cost to build, launch, and operate a mission through its prime mission, plus documented partner contributions, in 2025 dollars. Extended missions are not included. MethodsTracked publications: peer-reviewed papers we found that name the mission or its instruments in their titles, abstracts, or keywords. Papers that used the data without naming it are missed. MethodsCitations: how often other papers cited these publications. Each paper’s citations count over the year it appeared and the three years after, so older missions get no head start.
