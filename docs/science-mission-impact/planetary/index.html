Planetary Science

Planetary Science sends spacecraft to orbit, fly past, land on, rove across, strike, and bring back samples from other worlds. Most missions in this study went to Mars or the Moon; the rest went to comets, asteroids, and worlds from Mercury to Pluto. Many are mid-size Discovery missions led by a principal investigator, alongside flagships such as Cassini and a few CubeSats.

37 missions in this study, with 15,744 tracked publications and 584,598 citations to date.

Small and larger missions

Small missions, those that cost $150 million or less, make up 19% of Planetary Science’s missions in this study and 1.0% of its spending. They produced 0.96% of its top-10% papers, the ones other scientists cited most. Lunar Prospector alone produced 67% of that share. Larger missions also drew more citations per dollar.
