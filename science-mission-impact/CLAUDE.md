# Science Mission Impact

SvelteKit (Svelte 5) + `adapter-static` mini-site at `dashboards.planetary.org/science-mission-impact/`: a bibliometric analysis of the publications and citations produced by NASA science missions. Source in `science-mission-impact/`, prerendered output committed to `docs/science-mission-impact/` — **never hand-edit the output**; change the source and re-sync.

- **Data source:** the sibling `science-mission-citations` project's `dist/fixed/` dataset. Mission schema 6 and statistics schema 11 are recognized; configured run folders are `stats/{astro,bps,earth,helio,psd}`. Positional directories override `SMI_RAW_DIR`, which overrides the default; relative paths resolve from the app directory. Overrides name a dataset directory directly, with no automatic prefix or legacy/latest fallback. The gitignored `docs/science-mission-impact/dist/` is an older copy. The closing CLPS chart (`scripts/lib/clps.mjs` -> `site.clps`) reads a second dataset named explicitly by `smi.config.json` `clps.rawDir` (`dist/latest`; never a fallback), through the same verified loader; it must share the schema versions, its runs must share one as-of date at most a day from the main package's (the two exports are separate upstream runs, often an evening and the next morning; months close on the earlier date), and it is checked in `--check` preflight too. Raw data is never in CI, so **packaging runs locally only**.
- **Names, titles, dates:** `smi.config.json` `names` overrides a mission's published `name`/`fullName` everywhere (tiles, index, mission/division docs, CLPS, report); an id the package does not publish fails. Upstream `shortTitle` stays the key into every stats table. Paper titles and authors are cleaned of ADS markup (entities, SUB/SUP, MathML, TeX, processing instructions) by `cleanTitle` in `scripts/lib/text.mjs`, once on the `papers.json` rows plus the top-cited and curation refs. Durations read an unknown month in a date as 1 July; an unknown day stays the 1st, as upstream reads it (`parseDay` in `util.mjs`). `site.codeRevisionDirty` flags stats produced from a dirty upstream working tree (also in the report header).
- **Source checks:** `just smi-check [raw_dir]` (or `npm run data -- --check` in the app) validates manifests, selected mission identities, schemas, processing failures, and dashboard compatibility without writing files. Statistics select membership; the index is only an inventory. Companion paths are dataset-relative and their bytes/SHA-256 are checked before use.
- **Pipeline:** `just smi-refresh [raw_dir]` = read-only preflight → thumbnails → package data/report → tests → preview image → build → sync to `docs/`. Preflight validates all source references and packaging calculations before thumbnail changes or output cleanup. `--allow-broken-claims` is an accepted legacy no-op and bypasses no checks.
- **Three citation scopes, `full` by default:** every measure exists in the upstream `full_mission` scope (`full` here: the month after science starts through mission end + `run.full_mission_policy.post_prime_years`, cut at the latest mature end; `Site.fullPolicy`), the prime-phase `window` (`Site.windowPolicy`) and `lifetime`. The reader's view (`src/lib/state/view.svelte.js`, URL `?scope=`) defaults to `full`; top 1% exists in the two windowed scopes only. The by-year `LifetimeTimeline` follows the scope on division pages only: `fullSeries` on the division doc (`buildWindowSeries` in `scripts/lib/series.mjs`: the scope's papers once each, each cited from its publication year Y through Y + `citationYears`, a citing year before Y dropped as upstream's `WindowedPublication` drops it, checked against the scope's reported citations by `citationCoverage`); mission pages still show every paper to date, and the Key measures heading names the selected window's publication dates. A dataset without the `full_mission` scope or `run.full_mission_policy` fails preflight (regenerate upstream with `process.py --full-mission-window`). Reader-facing names: `full` is shown as 'Active Mission Window', `window` as 'Prime Mission Window' (`src/lib/copy/window.js`). The one `ScopeSwitch`, shared by the division and mission pages, offers only `SCOPES` from `view.svelte.js` (`full`, `lifetime`); `window` stays in the packaged docs only (no switch option, Methods section, CSV column or column in `static/data/papers/`, where a `window` view reads the full-mission columns), and a `?scope=window` link falls back to the default.
- **Analysis compatibility:** packaging uses each mission's exported publication cohorts and maturity status. Division accumulation reports changing measured-window coverage; unavailable output is never a measured zero. Cost comparisons and discussion figures derive from individual mission values, without importing cost-bin aggregates or legacy narrative claims.
- **Citation histories:** shared papers use the upstream representative rule (highest raw citation count, then title), never a union of competing histories, and every timeline reads a shared paper through that list, even for a mission whose own copy came back with no citations (ADS can give one paper different counts in queries seconds apart). Headline totals remain source-reported. A small missing set passes whatever the source declares: up to the larger of `citationHistory.maxMissing` and `maxMissingFraction` of each affected timeline's total (defaults: 5 and 0.001). Timelines label observed/expected/missing counts. Excess edges and larger shortfalls still fail preflight.
- **Recipes** (root `Justfile`): `smi-install` (npm ci), `smi-refresh [raw_dir] [--skip-thumbs] [--skip-build]` (whole pipeline), `smi-check` (read-only preflight), `smi-data` (package), `smi-thumbs` (cached mission thumbnails; `smi.config.json` `thumbnails.overrides` replaces a mission's upstream `image_url` before fetching and caching), `smi-test`, `smi-build` (build + sync), `smi-dev`.
- **Data-derived public figures:** the packager is the only producer — per-division shares, cost curves and 25–75% cost spans are computed from individual mission values in `package-data.mjs`, and every story figure by `buildDiscussion` in `scripts/lib/public.mjs` into `site.story` (`threshold` missions and mission papers at or under it, `failure` shortfall rates either side of it with `byDivision`, `comparison` either side of the threshold with failures as zeros plus per-group medians, kept apart per rankable division in `byDivision`, `largest` contributor, the equal-weight `pooledBand` from `pooledBand()` in `costcurve.mjs` with its `costShare`, `primer` the focus division's papers by citation count, the top-10% papers in each bar and the cutoff, per-division `missionShare`/`costShare`/`citationShare` of the small side, all over the same costed, measured missions, Wilson `interval`s and outright `lost` counts on `failure`, `perDollar` citations per $100M where `favors` is set only when the ordering survives dropping any one mission and `flipsOn` names the missions it does not survive dropping, plus `fit`, the threshold-free trend: least squares of log rate on log cost over the measured missions with citations (`logFit` in `scripts/lib/util.mjs`, the same regression the division scatter draws), with `tenfold` = 10^slope, `citesTenfold` = 10^(slope + 1) (the citations' own multiple, the same line before dividing by cost) and `holds` when no single dropped mission reverses its sign). Copy says "favors", "most" or names a mission only when the packaged fact says so. `src/lib/data/server.js` renders the generated JSON as-is; it recalculates nothing. Dates and distinct-paper totals retain their source provenance. The threshold is `smi.config.json` `thresholdCost` ($150M adjusted), published as `story.referenceCost`, with inclusive membership: under is `0 < cost <= thresholdCost`, over is `cost > thresholdCost` (`underThreshold()` in `public.mjs`). Two mission flags: `failed` (Failure only; failures count as zero output) and `shortfall` (Failure, Partial Failure or Partial Success; drives `failure`). Every mission row and scrolly tile carries `size` ('under', 'over' or null without a cost), stamped by the packager with `underThreshold`; pages and the story read it and never re-derive the line. Scrolly tiles are in launch order (`byLaunch`) and carry their own full-scope `top10` (plus `papersLifetime`/`citationsLifetime` for the corpus skyline) and `top10PerYear`, which the focus chart's bars read. `top10PerYear` (also on every mission row; `yearlyTopRate()` in `scripts/lib/measures.mjs`) is the lifetime top-10% credit of the papers published from the prime-mission start (launch as fallback) up to the data's end point, divided by the years between. The end point is never configured: it is the latest full-mission window end across the runs (`snapshot.dataEnd`), where upstream cuts the windows of missions still operating at its maturity ceiling. `story.rate` packages it (`end`, `through` the last publication year counted) and, per rankable division, the highest-rate mission and its side of the threshold, each side's count, missions with none and median rate, and the best small mission with how many larger ones it out-produced. The division the story walks through (primer and rate chart) is `smi.config.json` `storyDivision`; one that isn't rankable is a packaging warning, and its two steps (ONE, BARS) still render, blank, so later steps keep their numbers.
