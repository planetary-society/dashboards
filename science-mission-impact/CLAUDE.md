# Science Mission Impact

SvelteKit (Svelte 5) + `adapter-static` mini-site at `dashboards.planetary.org/science-mission-impact/`: a bibliometric analysis of the publications and citations produced by NASA science missions. Source in `science-mission-impact/`, prerendered output committed to `docs/science-mission-impact/` — **never hand-edit the output**; change the source and re-sync.

- **Data source:** the sibling `science-mission-citations` project's `dist/fixed/` dataset. Mission schema 5 and statistics schema 11 are recognized; configured run folders are `stats/{astro,bps,earth,helio,psd}`. Positional directories override `SMI_RAW_DIR`, which overrides the default; relative paths resolve from the app directory. Overrides name a dataset directory directly, with no automatic prefix or legacy/latest fallback. The gitignored `docs/science-mission-impact/dist/` is an older copy. The closing CLPS chart (`scripts/lib/clps.mjs` -> `site.clps`) reads a second dataset named explicitly by `smi.config.json` `clps.rawDir` (`dist/latest`; never a fallback), through the same verified loader; it must share the schema versions, its runs must share one as-of date at most a day from the main package's (the two exports are separate upstream runs, often an evening and the next morning; months close on the earlier date), and it is checked in `--check` preflight too. Raw data is never in CI, so **packaging runs locally only**.
- **Paper kinds:** an input file, `science-mission-impact/data/paper-kinds.json` (path in `smi.config.json` `paperKinds.file`), classifies every refereed paper of the missions at or under the threshold into six kinds (`results`, `data` count as science; `mission`, `review`, `future`, `other` do not); `scripts/lib/kinds.mjs` validates it and computes `site.kinds` in the full-mission scope. Membership is strict: an unclassified in-window paper of a mission at or under the threshold fails packaging (out-of-window ones only warn), a stale entry (unknown mission or bibcode) fails, and per-mission counts must equal the tile's. The two worked examples are `smi.config.json` `paperKinds.examples`; `paperKinds.smallMax` caps the small-corpora fact `site.kinds.small`. Each in-window row carries its share of the mission's top-10% credit (row weight ÷ missions sharing it), which must sum to the mission's `full.top10`; `site.kinds.topCredit` splits that credit by kind, with the non-science `share`.
- **Names, titles, dates:** `smi.config.json` `names` overrides a mission's published `name`/`fullName` everywhere (tiles, index, mission/division docs, kinds, CLPS, report); an id the package does not publish fails. Upstream `shortTitle` stays the key into every stats table. Paper titles and authors are cleaned of ADS markup (entities, SUB/SUP, MathML, TeX, processing instructions) by `cleanTitle` in `scripts/lib/text.mjs`, once on the `papers.json` rows plus the top-cited and curation refs. Durations read an unknown month in a date as 1 July; an unknown day stays the 1st, as upstream reads it (`parseDay` in `util.mjs`). `site.codeRevisionDirty` flags stats produced from a dirty upstream working tree (also in the report header).
- **Source checks:** `just smi-check [raw_dir]` (or `npm run data -- --check` in the app) validates manifests, selected mission identities, schemas, processing failures, and dashboard compatibility without writing files. Statistics select membership; the index is only an inventory. Companion paths are dataset-relative and their bytes/SHA-256 are checked before use.
- **Pipeline:** `just smi-refresh [raw_dir]` = read-only preflight → thumbnails → package data/report → tests → preview image → build → sync to `docs/`. Preflight validates all source references and packaging calculations before thumbnail changes or output cleanup. `--allow-broken-claims` is an accepted legacy no-op and bypasses no checks.
- **Three citation scopes, `full` by default:** every measure exists in the upstream `full_mission` scope (`full` here: the month after science starts through mission end + `run.full_mission_policy.post_prime_years`, cut at the latest mature end; `Site.fullPolicy`), the prime-phase `window` (`Site.windowPolicy`) and `lifetime`. The reader's view (`src/lib/state/view.svelte.js`, URL `?scope=`) defaults to `full`; top 1% exists in the two windowed scopes only. No chart draws a windowed timeline: the mission and division pages show the same by-year `LifetimeTimeline` (every paper to date), and the Key measures heading names the selected window's publication dates. A dataset without the `full_mission` scope or `run.full_mission_policy` fails preflight (regenerate upstream with `process.py --full-mission-window`). Reader-facing names: `full` is shown as 'Active Mission Window', `window` as 'Prime Mission Window' (`src/lib/copy/window.js`). The one `ScopeSwitch`, shared by the division and mission pages, offers only `SCOPES` from `view.svelte.js` (`full`, `lifetime`); `window` stays in the packaged docs only (no switch option, Methods section, CSV column or column in `static/data/papers/`, where a `window` view reads the full-mission columns), and a `?scope=window` link falls back to the default.
- **Analysis compatibility:** packaging uses each mission's exported publication cohorts and maturity status. Division accumulation reports changing measured-window coverage; unavailable output is never a measured zero. Cost comparisons and discussion figures derive from individual mission values, without importing cost-bin aggregates or legacy narrative claims.
- **Citation histories:** shared papers use the upstream representative rule (highest raw citation count, then title), never a union of competing histories, and every timeline reads a shared paper through that list, even for a mission whose own copy came back with no citations (ADS can give one paper different counts in queries seconds apart). Headline totals remain source-reported. A small missing set passes whatever the source declares: up to the larger of `citationHistory.maxMissing` and `maxMissingFraction` of each affected timeline's total (defaults: 5 and 0.001). Timelines label observed/expected/missing counts. Excess edges and larger shortfalls still fail preflight.
- **Recipes** (root `Justfile`): `smi-install` (npm ci), `smi-refresh [raw_dir] [--skip-thumbs] [--skip-build]` (whole pipeline), `smi-check` (read-only preflight), `smi-data` (package), `smi-thumbs` (cached mission thumbnails; `smi.config.json` `thumbnails.overrides` replaces a mission's upstream `image_url` before fetching and caching), `smi-test`, `smi-build` (build + sync), `smi-dev`.
- **Data-derived public figures:** the packager is the only producer — per-division shares, cost curves and 25–75% cost spans are computed from individual mission values in `package-data.mjs`, and every story figure by `buildDiscussion` in `scripts/lib/public.mjs` into `site.story` (`threshold` missions and mission papers at or under it, `failure` shortfall rates either side of it with `byDivision`, `comparison` either side of the threshold with failures as zeros plus per-group medians, kept apart per rankable division in `byDivision`, `largest` contributor, the equal-weight `pooledBand` from `pooledBand()` in `costcurve.mjs` with its `costShare`, `primer` the focus division's papers by citation count, the top-10% papers in each bar and the cutoff, per-division `missionShare`/`costShare`/`citationShare` of the small side, all over the same costed, measured missions (the overview's per-dollar lanes read them against each other), Wilson `interval`s and outright `lost` counts on `failure`, `perDollar` citations per $100M where `favors` is set only when the ordering survives dropping any one mission and `flipsOn` names the missions it does not survive dropping, plus `fit`, the threshold-free trend: least squares of log rate on log cost over the measured missions with citations (`logFit` in `scripts/lib/util.mjs`, the same regression the division scatter draws), with `tenfold` = 10^slope and `holds` when no single dropped mission reverses its sign). Copy says "favors", "most" or names a mission only when the packaged fact says so. `src/lib/data/server.js` renders the generated JSON as-is; it recalculates nothing. Dates and distinct-paper totals retain their source provenance. The threshold is `smi.config.json` `thresholdCost` ($150M adjusted), published as `story.referenceCost`, with inclusive membership: under is `0 < cost <= thresholdCost`, over is `cost > thresholdCost` (`underThreshold()` in `public.mjs`). Two mission flags: `failed` (Failure only; failures count as zero output) and `shortfall` (Failure, Partial Failure or Partial Success; drives `failure`). Every mission row and scrolly tile carries `size` ('under', 'over' or null without a cost), stamped by the packager with `underThreshold`; pages and the story read it and never re-derive the line. Scrolly tiles are in launch order (`byLaunch`) and carry their own full-scope `top10` (plus `papersLifetime`/`citationsLifetime` for the corpus skyline), which the running total's bars read, and only `first[scope].state` of their first top paper. The division the story walks through (primer and running total) is `smi.config.json` `storyDivision`; one that isn't rankable is a packaging warning, and its three steps (ONE, BARS, RUNNING) still render, blank, so later steps keep their numbers.
- **Story arc** (`src/lib/scrolly/Story.svelte`: a title step and twenty-six story steps over one stage of mission squares, constants `TITLE`…`RECAP`, the close sharing RECAP's graphic; read the file for the step order). One argument: very small missions can't make up for the science of larger ones; detail belongs on the division pages (`src/lib/copy/discussion.js`) and qualifiers in Methods. Right after the missions (`OVERVIEW`), one `FINDINGS` box states all three findings over a full-bleed photo (`data-bleed`, like MOTIVATION; the stage is hidden under it), before any definitions, so it glosses the terms it uses ("small", "top-10% papers"); each section opens with its `KICKERS` line, numbered as in the box. Then "How we measured it" introduces each concept before the chart that needs it: the corpus skyline, which zooms out from papers to citations on one √ scale, then a citation primer on the focus division and the top-10% papers. Finding 1, small and larger: the $150M split as icon arrays, then the same comparison by division (`VARY`, `VaryChart`), the focus division's running total built up as axis → bars → stacked total, then per dollar mission by mission: the focus division's log-log scatter, then one line per step: the one it would follow if small missions did better (`WOULD_SLOPE`), the same line swung flat for missions that did as well, then its packaged fit taking the flat line's place and swinging up off it. Finding 2, the cost floor: `FLOORS` marks on the division lanes the cost by which each division's cheapest quarter of top-10% papers came in (`story.bands` p25), then the small side's share (`ALL`) and per dollar (`DOLLAR`), then every compared division's fit in bands on one shared rate scale (`FITS`). Finding 3: CLPS. Then `RECAP`, the three findings alone in bold over the launch grid, and the close. Rules the steps must keep:
  - **Copy follows packaged facts only.** Comparison says "each"/"fewer" only when the under side is lower in every division; the REACHED step reads `comparison.groups` (`withTop`, `failed`) and says "most" only when more than half the small missions never had a top-10% paper; per-dollar says "favor" only from `favors`, and a trend "rises" or "falls" only from `fit.slope`, is "nearly flat" when `fit.tenfold` is within 0.25 of 1, carries "leaving out a single mission tips it the other way" when `!fit.holds`, and is bold across divisions only when every one rises and holds; a close division names its largest small mission (plus the dependency when `flipsOn` is that one mission); a mission is named as carrying the small side, and a per-dollar lead as resting on one mission, only by `carrier`/`loneFlip` in `src/lib/copy/discussion.js`; the trend words come from its `flat`/`direction`/`times`, which the division pages share; CLPS says "fewer", and the third finding "not matching", only when `clps.comparison.behindAll`; the first finding (`FINDING_ONE`, "can't make up for", in `FINDINGS` and `RECAP`) needs `verdict`: fewer top-10% papers per mission in every division (`comparison.byDivision`) and fewer citations per dollar in most (`perDollar.favors`); the close's evidence after its concession leads with per dollar, and failures there are pooled, never per division. the second finding (`FINDING_TWO`, "no single cost floor", in `FINDINGS`, `FLOORS` and `RECAP`) needs `floorSpread`, the highest packaged p25 at least twice the lowest, and its evidence names the lowest and highest; per-division small-mission failure rates and shares with a top-10% paper don't differ beyond chance at these sample sizes, so neither is stated as a division difference; `VARY` lists failures per division as counts, says differences this size could be chance, and says "in every division" only when larger missions more often had a top-10% paper in each (`reachEvery`); `FITS` gives tenfold effect sizes only for `firm` fits (not nearly flat, holding). SPLIT defines "small missions" (at or under the threshold; `FINDINGS` glosses it before that); later prose says "small" and "larger".
  - **Fixed sources:** the motivation and findings photos live in `static/img/story/`, never `img/missions/` (the findings photo is NASA's public-domain iss074e0301636, credited "NASA"); the kinds example comes from `smi.config.json paperKinds.examples`, never a hard-coded id, and lists every classified paper with lifetime citations while the bars beside it stay in-window.
  - **Layout:** paired steps share one graphic state (`PAPERS`/`TOP` the primer histogram, grey then the top 10% in blue; `SPLIT`/`FAILURE`/`REACHED` the icon arrays, REACHED keeping every square in place and washing blue those with a top-10% paper; `FLOORS`/`ALL`/`DOLLAR` the division lanes, FLOORS marking each line's quarter at the packaged p25, DOLLAR the citations and spending lines in place of top papers; `SCATTER`/`DOWN`/`FLAT`/`FIT` the focus division's per-dollar scatter, never more than one line on it; `FITS` the per-dollar bands; `VARY` alone `VaryChart`, small against larger shares that failed or fell short and that had a top-10% paper, by division (rings small, dots larger, no blue); `CLPS`/`CLPS_COMPARE` the CLPS chart; `RECAP`/`CLOSE` the launch grid; `KINDS`/`KINDS_ALL`, hidden, between `FIT` and `FLOORS`), so long text is split across steps; each step's text must fit a 390px phone (under ~330px tall). `--step-gap` in `Scroller.svelte` separates steps, and `Scroller` marks a step whose text doesn't fit `.overflows` (text goes `position: static` and scrolls). Every `svh` length has a plain `vh` fallback. Spaces around `{#if}` blocks go outside them (Svelte trims whitespace at a block's edges). Scrubbed builds (`span(a, b)` over a step's progress) run while the text holds; reduced motion renders every scrubbed graphic complete. Prose money is `moneyWords()` ("$150 million", TPS style), chart labels `money()` ("$150M").
  - **Squares** (`Squares.svelte`): every square keeps its mission thumbnail in every step; states draw over the picture (bright/dimmed/colour wash), and hairlines go on an overlay above the image, since an inset shadow would paint under it. Paper-kind colours are the `.kind-*` classes in `app.css` (science kinds in the two blues, the other four in greys about 21 L* apart).
  - **Analysis:** cross-division figures weight divisions equally or keep them apart; top-paper credit is never summed across fields. Missing costs and unavailable output stay distinct from measured zero. The $150M threshold is an editorial line, not a finding; no optimal price is inferred.
- **Division pages** (`src/routes/[division]/`): (1) `divisionIntro` in `src/lib/copy/divisions.js` (what the division does and the kinds of missions it flies, editorial and count-free so a refresh can't stale it) and `MissionThumbs`, every mission's picture in launch order. (2) Under the sticky `ScopeSwitch`, which every figure below it follows, "Output and cost" from `site.divisionFacts[slug][scope]` (`divisionFacts()` in `public.mjs`: the division's own story rows, read in each scope by `buildDiscussion`): the lede (`statsLede` in `src/lib/copy/discussion.js`: the whole division's missions, cost and top-10% papers, then its per-dollar fit worded by the story's own trend rules; no small-vs-larger framing except for a division with nothing to compare); the summary table (`statRows`: one division-wide column, each figure the sum of the packaged under and over sides, citations per $100M over the pooled cost) beside the by-year `LifetimeTimeline`, which is every paper to date whatever the scope; then `PerDollarScatter`, which is the overview's single-division chart (`perDollarLayout` + `DollarChart` + `Squares`) in the page's scope with the scope's packaged `perDollar.fit` and no threshold line (`DollarChart` draws the $150M line only when given `referenceCost`). Here and on the overview, `DollarChart` draws a fit that is nearly flat or reversed by leaving out one mission as a dashed grey line, with "Nearly flat" in a band's label when flat: the copy's own rule. A division with nothing to compare gets the lede and timeline only. (3) `MissionTable`: every mission in one list, earliest launch first (`launchDate` joined from the scrolly tiles in `+page.server.js`), with thumbnails, and a CSV (`missionsCsv` in `src/lib/mission/papers.js`) carrying both selectable scopes (`full`, `lifetime`), both tiers and h/g. Cost everywhere is `adjusted_lcc` (prime-mission life-cycle cost with partners); `adjusted_extended_spending`/`adjusted_full_mission_cost` are never read. One definition per "i", from `FOOTNOTES`.
- **Mission pages** open with Mission facts (`missionFactGroups` in `src/lib/copy/measures.js`, rendered by `MeasureTable`): the catalog's program, type, target, status, launch date and vehicle, prime mission and end dates (a blank one drops out), and life-cycle cost both as reported (`meta.lcc`, upstream `lcc`, nominal) and adjusted (`meta.cost`, `adjusted_lcc`).
- **Affiliation maps** ("Where the authors worked", `AffiliationMap.svelte`): every mission page after its by-year chart and every division page after the scoped sections, lifetime only (no scope), with a US/World toggle (`Choice`, local state; a mission with no US author opens on the world). `scripts/lib/affiliations.mjs` bins each packaged paper's `institution_ids` (records) located by the mission's `institutions` companion (ADS canonical affiliations, ROR coordinates) on each map's grid; a cell counts a paper once, a division counts each of its papers once across missions, and an unknown institution id fails packaging. The doc carries `affiliations` (papers, mapped per map, top three institutions); the cells go to `static/data/maps/{missions,divisions}/<id>.json`, fetched after load like the paper list. Projections, grids and US membership (US institutions `geoAlbersUsa` can place, so not Puerto Rico or the Virgin Islands) live in `src/lib/charts/affiliationmap.js`, shared by the packager, the page and `scripts/basemaps.mjs`, which draws the land into `static/img/maps/{us,world}.svg`; rerun it after changing a projection (`scripts/tests/basemaps.test.mjs` fails until the committed files match).
- **Secondary measures:** h-index, i100 and a dated m-index chart are shown for readers of the accompanying paper; they never drive the story. m-index falls every 1 January, so it is always printed with the data's as-of date.
- **Analysis rules:** percentiles and shares are always within-division; the full-mission window is the default scope for every comparison. High impact = top 10% of the division's whole corpus in the scope, every publication year ranked together (`<prefix>_top_k["10"]` and the papers' `top10_weight`/`window_top10_weight`; never the year-cohort `*_cohort_top10` fields); top 1% is the same ranking at 1%, in the windowed scopes only. Cost curves exclude missing/nonpositive costs from both denominators.
- **Gitignore traps:** the root `.gitignore` (a Python template) ignores `dist/`, `build/` and `lib/` at any depth — the app's own `.gitignore` re-includes `src/lib/` and `scripts/lib/`, and SvelteKit builds to `.site/` rather than `build/`.
