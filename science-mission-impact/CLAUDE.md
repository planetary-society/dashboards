# Science Mission Impact

SvelteKit (Svelte 5) + `adapter-static` mini-site at `dashboards.planetary.org/science-mission-impact/`: a bibliometric analysis of the publications and citations produced by NASA science missions. Source in `science-mission-impact/`, prerendered output committed to `docs/science-mission-impact/` — **never hand-edit the output**; change the source and re-sync.

- **Data source:** the sibling `science-mission-citations` project's `dist/fixed/` dataset. Mission schema 6 and statistics schema 11 are recognized; configured run folders are `stats/{astro,bps,earth,helio,psd}`. Positional directories override `SMI_RAW_DIR`, which overrides the default; relative paths resolve from the app directory. Overrides name a dataset directory directly, with no automatic prefix or fallback to another dataset. The gitignored `docs/science-mission-impact/dist/` is an older copy. The story's CLPS chart and Analysis Figure 7 (`scripts/lib/clps.mjs` -> `site.clps`) reads a second dataset named explicitly by `smi.config.json` `clps.rawDir` (`dist/latest`; never a fallback), through the same verified loader; it must share the schema versions, its runs must share one as-of date at most a day from the main package's (the two exports are separate upstream runs, often an evening and the next morning; months close on the earlier date), and it is checked in `--check` preflight too. Raw data is never in CI, so **packaging runs locally only**.
- **Names, titles, dates:** `smi.config.json` `names` overrides a mission's published `name`/`fullName` everywhere (tiles, index, mission/division docs, CLPS, report); an id the package does not publish fails. Upstream `shortTitle` stays the key into every stats table. Paper titles and authors are cleaned of ADS markup (entities, SUB/SUP, MathML, TeX, processing instructions) by `cleanTitle` in `scripts/lib/text.mjs`, once on the `papers.json` rows plus the top-cited and curation refs. Durations read an unknown month in a date as 1 July; an unknown day stays the 1st, as upstream reads it (`parseDay` in `util.mjs`). `site.codeRevisionDirty` flags stats produced from a dirty upstream working tree (also in the report header).
- **Source checks:** `just smi-check [raw_dir]` (or `npm run data -- --check` in the app) validates manifests, selected mission identities, schemas, processing failures, and dashboard compatibility without writing files. Statistics select membership; the index is only an inventory. Companion paths are dataset-relative and their bytes/SHA-256 are checked before use.
- **Pipeline:** `just smi-refresh [raw_dir]` = read-only preflight → thumbnails → package data/report → tests → preview image → build → sync to `docs/`. Preflight validates all source references and packaging calculations before thumbnail changes or output cleanup. `--allow-broken-claims` is accepted and does nothing.
- **Three citation scopes, `full` by default:** every measure exists in the upstream `full_mission` scope (`full` here: the month after science starts through mission end + `run.full_mission_policy.post_prime_years`, cut at the latest mature end; `Site.fullPolicy`), the prime-phase `window` (`Site.windowPolicy`) and `lifetime`. The reader's view (`src/lib/state/view.svelte.js`, URL `?scope=`) defaults to `full`; top 1% exists in the two windowed scopes only. The by-year `LifetimeTimeline` follows the scope on division pages only: `fullSeries` on the division doc (`buildWindowSeries` in `scripts/lib/series.mjs`: the scope's papers once each, each cited from its publication year Y through Y + `citationYears`, a citing year before Y dropped as upstream's `WindowedPublication` drops it, checked against the scope's reported citations by `citationCoverage`); mission pages still show every paper to date, and their `ScopeSwitch` hints add the mission's own dates (`scopeSpans`: the full window's exported bounds, the lifetime's first publication year to the as-of date). A dataset without the `full_mission` scope or `run.full_mission_policy` fails preflight (regenerate upstream with `process.py --full-mission-window`). Reader-facing names: `full` is shown as 'Active Mission Window', `window` as 'Prime Mission Window' (`src/lib/copy/window.js`). The one `ScopeSwitch`, shared by the division and mission pages, offers only `SCOPES` from `view.svelte.js` (`full`, `lifetime`); `window` stays in the packaged docs only (no switch option, Methods section, CSV column or column in `static/data/papers/`, where a `window` view reads the full-mission columns), and a `?scope=window` link falls back to the default.
- **Analysis compatibility:** packaging uses each mission's exported publication cohorts and maturity status. Division accumulation reports changing measured-window coverage; unavailable output is never a measured zero. Cost comparisons and discussion figures derive from individual mission values, without importing upstream cost-bin aggregates or narrative claims.
- **Citation histories:** shared papers use the upstream representative rule (highest raw citation count, then title), never a union of competing histories, and every timeline reads a shared paper through that list, even for a mission whose own copy came back with no citations (ADS can give one paper different counts in queries seconds apart). Headline totals remain source-reported. A small missing set passes whatever the source declares: up to the larger of `citationHistory.maxMissing` and `maxMissingFraction` of each affected timeline's total (defaults: 5 and 0.001). Timelines label observed/expected/missing counts. Excess edges and larger shortfalls still fail preflight.
- **Recipes** (root `Justfile`): `smi-install` (npm ci), `smi-refresh [raw_dir] [--skip-thumbs] [--skip-build]` (whole pipeline), `smi-check` (read-only preflight), `smi-data` (package), `smi-thumbs` (cached mission thumbnails; `smi.config.json` `thumbnails.overrides` replaces a mission's upstream `image_url` before fetching and caching), `smi-test`, `smi-build` (build + sync), `smi-dev`.
- **Data-derived public figures:** the packager is the only producer — per-division shares, cost curves and 25–75% cost spans are computed from individual mission values in `package-data.mjs`, and every story figure by `buildDiscussion` in `scripts/lib/public.mjs` into `site.story` (`threshold` missions and mission papers at or under it; `failure` shortfall rates either side of it with Wilson `interval`s, outright `lost` counts, `higher`, a two-sided Fisher exact `fisherP` (`fisherP` in `scripts/lib/util.mjs`, shared with `analysis.mjs`) and `byDivision`; `comparison` either side of the threshold with failures as zeros plus per-group medians, kept apart per rankable division in `byDivision`; the `largest` contributor; the equal-weight `pooledBand` from `pooledBand()` in `costcurve.mjs` with its `costShare`; `primer` the focus division's papers by citation count, the top-10% papers in each bar and the cutoff; per-division `missionShare`/`costShare`/`citationShare` of the small side; `perDollar` citations per $100M either side, where `favors` is set only when the ordering survives dropping any one mission and `flipsOn` names the missions it does not survive dropping, plus `runningMean`). **Failure rates count the same missions as the citation figures:** every costed packaged mission, measured or not (`story.failure`, `analysis.failures`). Upstream admits a zero-output failure two years after it ends but a success only once its prime window matures, so among the latest launches a failure can be counted before a success. **Citation membership follows the upstream fixed set:** every selected mission stays eligible for citation comparisons, including failures; each measure still requires its own available output and positive known cost where relevant. Upstream uses ordinary prime-window maturity, with a validated zero-output failure exception two years after mission end. No dashboard launch-date cutoff is applied. Generated copy says "favors", "most" or names a mission only when the packaged fact says so; the overview story's fixed sentences that make such claims are listed under **Story arc**. `src/lib/data/server.js` renders the generated JSON as-is; it recalculates nothing. Dates and distinct-paper totals retain their source provenance. The threshold is `smi.config.json` `thresholdCost` ($150M adjusted), published as `story.referenceCost`, with inclusive membership: under is `0 < cost <= thresholdCost`, over is `cost > thresholdCost` (`underThreshold()` in `public.mjs`). Two mission flags: `failed` (Failure only; failures count as zero output) and `shortfall` (Failure, Partial Failure or Partial Success; drives `failure`). Every mission row and scrolly tile carries `size` ('under', 'over' or null without a cost), stamped by the packager with `underThreshold`; pages read it and never re-derive the line, and `src/routes/+page.server.js` leaves it off the story's tiles (the story takes each side's figures from `site.story`). Scrolly tiles are in launch order (`byLaunch`) and carry their own full-scope `top10` (plus `papersLifetime`/`citationsLifetime` for the corpus skyline, an illustration) and `top10PerYear`, which the focus chart's bars read. `top10PerYear` (also on every mission row; `yearlyTopRate()` in `scripts/lib/measures.mjs`) is the mission's full-scope top-10% credit divided by its full window's length in years: the same fixed-window ranking whose cutoff the primer shows. No comparison of citations ever reads a lifetime statistic (Casey's rule); lifetime totals appear only as illustration (the skyline) and in time to science (his choice). `story.rate` packages, per rankable division, the highest-rate mission and its side of the threshold, each side's count, missions with none and median rate, and the best small mission with how many larger ones it out-produced. The division the story walks through (primer and rate chart) is `smi.config.json` `storyDivision`; one that isn't rankable is a packaging warning, and its two steps (ONE, BARS) still render, blank, so later steps keep their numbers.
- **Overview running averages:** each `perDollar.runningMean` carries 81 [cost, mean rate, lower rate, upper rate] samples, with null values where support is inadequate. It averages individual missions' citations per $100M, including recorded zeros, using tricube weights on log10 cost within one decade each way (factor ten). Require three contributing missions and Kish effective n >= 2 (allow unequal weights among three neighbours while rejecting windows dominated by one mission); do not extrapolate or bridge missing samples. Seeded 2,000 mission-level bootstrap resamples give pointwise 95% percentile intervals with the bandwidth fixed; zero bounds stay zero, and invalid empty windows are omitted only while at least 95% of resamples remain valid. These are mean intervals, not prediction or simultaneous bands. A separate `runningMean.comparison` compares arithmetic mean rates at the predefined threshold: small minus larger, stratified bootstrap 95% interval, and leave-one-out sign stability. `favors` requires an interval wholly on one side of zero and stable ordering, with at least three missions per group. This is distinct from `perDollar.favors`, the pooled-citation/spending comparison. The overview and division pages use these running means; the overview headline uses the separate group comparisons.

- **Secondary measures:** h-index, i100 and a dated m-index chart are shown for readers of the accompanying paper; they never drive the story. m-index falls every 1 January, so it is always printed with the data's as-of date.
- **Analysis rules:** percentiles and shares are always within-division; the full-mission window is the default scope for every comparison. High impact = top 10% of the division's whole corpus in the scope, every publication year ranked together (`<prefix>_top_k["10"]` and the papers' `top10_weight`/`window_top10_weight`; never the year-cohort `*_cohort_top10` fields); top 1% is the same ranking at 1%, in the windowed scopes only. Cost curves exclude missing/nonpositive costs from both denominators.
